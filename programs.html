The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 12 weeks, all built around episodes you actually publish.

Program 01 · A format you can sustain

Launch your show

Most shows are designed for an enthusiastic host with a free Saturday. We design yours for a busy Tuesday: a format with a realistic length, a clear promise to one specific listener, and the first three episodes recorded before you launch.

  • Not launched yet
  • 5 hours a week
  • A microphone

Program 02 · Stop reading your questions

Interview skills

A good interview is mostly listening and one brave follow-up. We work on preparation that leaves room for surprises, the art of the second question, and being comfortable with silence — which is where guests say the interesting thing.

  • 8 weeks
  • Interview shows
  • 4 hours a week
  • Guests booked

Start this program

Program 03 · Good enough, fast

Sound & editing

Listeners forgive average content far more readily than bad audio. We fix recording first — mic technique, a quieter room, correct levels — then build an editing routine with a defined finish line, because editing perfectionism kills more shows than bad microphones.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any level
  • 4 hours a week
  • Your current setup

Program 04 · Beyond your friends

Audience & sponsors

Podcast growth is slow and mostly comes from other shows and word of mouth. We work on being findable, on the clips and show notes that travel, and on reading your analytics without lying to yourself. If sponsorship is the goal, we build an honest media kit — no inflated numbers.

  • 10+ episodes
  • 4 hours a week
  • Real analytics
